The face is MediaPipe's canonical face model with a modelled head. Without a camera it sways and speaks on its own. In the browser, Use camera runs MediaPipe face tracking on the front camera and the microphone. In the Facefield iPhone app, ARKit's face mesh, blendshapes and the TrueDepth depth map drive the same shaders: the pin-screen shows measured depth, hair and shoulders included.
